## Lead Quality Verdict

BedHead PJs is a good `B` or `B+` lead, but not an A+ exporter lead by itself.

Why it is useful:

- Real apparel/sleepwear brand.
- Real product fit for textile/apparel exporters.
- Real wholesale page.
- Real decision-relevant inbox exists: `wholesale@bedheadpjs.com`.
- Parent company Komar appears to have broader sourcing/manufacturing/distribution operations.

Why it is not A+:

- The visible wholesale path appears sales-side: BedHead wants retailers/stores to apply to sell BedHead products.
- The scraper did not find a direct procurement, sourcing, vendor onboarding, or supplier application contact.
- `wholesale@bedheadpjs.com` may route to sales/channel management, not sourcing.
- The better target is likely Komar's sourcing/procurement team, not BedHead's retail wholesale team.

## Outreach Recommendation

Do contact this lead, but do not treat it as top-priority A+ unless a Komar-side sourcing/procurement contact is found.

Best contact order:

1. Komar sourcing/procurement contact, if discoverable.
2. `sara.wakefield@komarbrands.com`
3. `wholesale@bedheadpjs.com`

Suggested angle:

> Sleepwear/loungewear manufacturer with organic cotton, woven/knit pajama, private-label, sampling, and export capacity.

The outreach should not sound like a generic textile supplier pitch. It should be tied specifically to sleepwear, pajamas, cotton knits/wovens, quality control, compliance, and reliable production capacity.

## Scoring Implication

This result exposed an important scoring distinction:

- `wholesale account` can mean the company sells wholesale to retailers.
- It does not always mean the company buys from exporters or needs new suppliers.

For A+ scoring, the scraper should require stronger buyer-side evidence such as:

- `vendor application`
- `supplier portal`
- `supplier application`
- `procurement`
- `sourcing`
- `purchasing`
- `importer`
- `private label production inquiry`
- parent-company sourcing contact
- import/distribution evidence

## Final Judgment

Keep BedHead PJs in the database as a usable lead, but mark it as:

- Lead tier: `B`
- Contactability: strong
- Product fit: strong
- Buyer/procurement certainty: moderate
- Close potential: moderate unless Komar sourcing contact is found

It should not count as an A+ lead for measuring time-to-perfect-lead.
