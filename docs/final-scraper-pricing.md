# Final Scraper Pricing

## Recommended Offer

Start with:

```text
$50 = 10 verified apparel/textile buyer leads
```

Do not sell this as raw scraped data. The value is in the qualification, evidence, and contact route.

## What Counts As A Verified Lead

Each paid lead should include:

1. Company name and domain.
2. Website.
3. Buyer-side evidence.
4. Evidence URL.
5. Contact route.
6. Outreach contact.
7. Lead score and tier.
8. Why it qualified.
9. Suggested pitch angle.
10. Closeability notes.

## Suggested Packages

1. `$50`: 10 verified leads.
2. `$100`: 25 verified leads.
3. `$200`: 60 verified leads.
4. `$50`: 5 A+ only leads.
5. Custom: A+ leads at `$8-$15` per lead.

## Best Positioning

Use this positioning:

```text
Verified apparel/textile buyer leads with evidence and contact route.
```

Avoid this positioning:

```text
100 scraped leads for $50.
```

That makes the product look like a cheap list instead of a useful sales tool.

## Replacement Guarantee

Offer a simple quality guarantee:

```text
If a lead is clearly not buyer-relevant, it is replaced.
```

This makes the $50 offer easier to buy and forces the scraper output to stay strict.

## Current Product Verdict

At the current quality level, the scraper is sellable at `$50` for `10` verified leads.

If the buyer wants only A+ leads, sell fewer leads:

```text
$50 = 5 A+ leads
```

That is more defensible because A+ leads require stronger evidence and take longer to find.
